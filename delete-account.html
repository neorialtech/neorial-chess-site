Delete your account

In the app (fastest)

Open Settings, scroll to Your data, press Delete my account and confirm. Your account, sign-in details, friend list, blocks and friend code are removed at once. Games you played against other people are kept without your name so that your opponent's history stays complete.

If you can no longer open the app

Write to support@neorial.com from any address, give your display name and (if you know it) your friend code, and we will delete the account within 30 days. We may ask a question only you could answer.

खाता हटाना

ऐप में Settings → Your data → Delete my account दबाएँ। यदि आप ऐप नहीं खोल पा रहे हैं तो support@neorial.com पर लिखें; हम 30 दिनों के भीतर खाता हटा देंगे।
